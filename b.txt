Who Knows One? (Echad Mi Yodea)
Who knows one?
I know one.
One is our God, in the Heaven and the Earth.

Who knows two?
I know two.
Two are the tablets of the covenant;
One is our God, in the Heaven and the Earth.

Who knows three?
I know three.
Three are the patriarchs (Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob);
Two are the tablets of the covenant;
One is our God, in the Heaven and the Earth.

Who knows four?
I know four.
Four are the matriarchs (Sarah, Rebecca, Rachel, and Leah);
Three are the patriarchs...

Who knows five?
I know five.
Five are the books of the Torah;
Four are the matriarchs...

Who knows six?
I know six.
Six are the sections of the Mishnah;
Five are the books of the Torah...

Who knows seven?
I know seven.
Seven are the days of the week;
Six are the sections of the Mishnah...

Who knows eight?
I know eight.
Eight are the days until circumcision (Brit Milah);
Seven are the days of the week...

Who knows nine?
I know nine.
Nine are the months of pregnancy;
Eight are the days until circumcision...
